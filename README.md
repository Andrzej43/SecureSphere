## What is Hoops?

Hoops is a fast-paced multiplayer basketball game built on the Modd.io platform.  
Jump into 2v2 matches, shoot from anywhere on the court, defend the paint, and race to the win condition.

It’s designed for quick sessions — easy to pick up, hard to master.

---

## Gameplay

- **Format**: 2v2 basketball
- **Scoring**: 2-point and 3-point shots
- **Win Condition**: First team to 11 points
- Physics-based shooting (distance, defenders, and positioning affect accuracy)
- Automatic dribbling mechanics
- Team passing and ball movement
- Real-time multiplayer with rollback netcode for responsive play

---
